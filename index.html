Islamabad,The Beautiful

Islamabad, the purpose-built capital of Pakistan, sits at the northern edge of the Pothohar Plateau against the lush backdrop of the Margalla Hills. Designed in the 1960s by Greek architect Constantinos Apostolou Doxiadis, the city is known for its organized grid of tree-lined sectors, abundant green spaces, and tranquil atmosphere compared to Pakistan's older metropolises. As the country's political and diplomatic center, it houses key government institutions alongside striking modern landmarks such as the tent-shaped Faisal Mosque, the Pakistan Monument, and Daman-e-Koh, which offers sweeping panoramic views over the capital.
